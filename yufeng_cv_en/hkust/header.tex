\section{Profile}
  \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item\small{MSc by Research student at the Institute for Computing Systems Architecture (ICSA), University of Edinburgh, supervised by Prof.\ Mahesh K.\ Marina. My work sits at the intersection of \textbf{machine learning systems} and \textbf{networked systems}: how a training or inference workload shares an accelerator and a network with a latency-critical tenant, and how such systems are measured at a scale no physical testbed can reach.
    \par\vspace{3pt}
    Two lines run through everything I have built. The first is \textbf{resource sharing under a hard deadline} --- deciding how much GPU capacity an AI workload may harvest from infrastructure it does not own, on a control-loop timescale set by the priority tenant, and keeping the harvesting workload's state intact when that capacity is reclaimed. The second is the \textbf{high-performance data plane} underneath: I own the C++ inter-device transport in \textit{Morphling} (event-driven I/O, zero-copy scatter-gather, pinned-memory pooling, serialization-cost analysis), where end-to-end fidelity depends on microsecond-level transfer behaviour. This work has produced papers at ACM MobiCom and a Best Paper award at EdgeSys'26.
    \par\vspace{3pt}
    \textbf{I am applying for the PhD programme at iSING Lab for Fall 2027 entry, and intend to apply to the Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme.} What draws me to the lab is the \textbf{AI-centric networking} and \textbf{machine learning systems} directions: I have worked on the compute side of AI infrastructure sharing and on the transport layer that carries it, and I want to push the same questions down into the datacenter network itself --- RDMA, SmartNICs and programmable switches --- rather than treating the network as a given.}
  \end{itemize}
